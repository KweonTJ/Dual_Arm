The AX body and B01 horn are assembly reference solids. The guide, carriage block and bearings are interface envelopes. The 145 mm rail-hole pattern is a design assumption for the order drawing. The catalog 155 mm rail is supplied as the original comparison only.

CAD/centered_hand.py uses CadQuery 2.7 and numpy. parts() returns individual solids, state(gap_mm) gives rigid transforms in the existing wrist frame and shapes(gap_mm) places the assembly. Centering, mounting patterns and the stroke are implemented in these functions. STEP files in Parts/ and Reference/ are the kept inputs for the unchanged components. CAD/Baseline/Parts contains both source jaw solids in the legacy local frame.
